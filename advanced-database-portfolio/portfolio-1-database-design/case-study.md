# Organisation: ROO’s
#### An imaginary cloud electronic medical records (EMR) platform used by home health agencies.

## Nature of system
ROO's is a multi-tenant EMR platform. Each agency’s patients, clinicians, visits and billing are kept separate.

## Users: 
* Agency admins 
* Schedulers 
* Nurses 
    *  Registered Nurse (RN)
    * Licensed Vocational Nurse (LVN)
* Therapists
* Home health aides (HHA) 
* Billers
* Physicians (read and sign orders).

## Major data
- Patients
- Care episodes
- Physician orders, 
- Scheduled and completed visits 
- Visit notes 
- Vital signs
- Medications
- Claims 
- Payments


## Key operations 
- Admit a patient
- Open an episode 
- Schedule and complete visits 
- Record vitals and notes 
- Sign physician orders 
- Maintain medication lists 
- Generate claims and record payments
- Retrieve a patient’s full history.
